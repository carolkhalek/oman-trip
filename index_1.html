How the plan works for all four of us

The group always sleeps at the same place. On activity days the friends go out with the guide or on foot, and Carol has a short, flat option nearby.

Shared every day

The drive, meals, sunsets and every evening and overnight stop.

Split on activity days

Climbing, Snake Canyon, the Balcony Walk and Wadi Shab are for the friends. Carol has a nearby alternative each time.

One night of camping together

All four camp at the Wahiba Sands desert camp on Thu Nov 12. On Jebel Shams the friends camp and Carol takes a chalet on the same site.

Easy outdoor stops for everyone

The Jebel Shams rim viewpoint, the Wadi Bani Khalid pools, Bimmah Sinkhole and turtle watching need only short, flat walks.

Questions to send the guide

    Car and the Mezyad border crossing

    Rent a high-clearance 4WD in Dubai for the whole week, with the rental company's written permission to drive it into Oman. The roads to Jebel Shams and Balad Sayt have steep, unpaved sections.

    At the border

    • UAE exit fee: AED 35 per person.
    • Insurance: confirm the rental includes Oman cover. If not, buy an Orange Card at the border, about AED 106 for at least 5 days (third-party only).
    • Documents: passports valid 6+ months, driving licences, car registration card and the rental company's permission letter.
    • Visas: check Oman's entry rules for each passport before the trip.
    • Confirm Mezyad opening hours for Nov 8 and Nov 15.

    Source: Gulf News, UAE to Oman by road

    Weather and packing

    • About 25–30°C on the coast and in the desert in mid-November.
    • About 10°C at night on Jebel Shams: bring a warm layer.
    • Water shoes and dry bags for the wadis.
    • Swimwear that covers shoulders and knees for public wadis.
    • Grand Mosque for women: headscarf, long sleeves, ankle-length clothing.
    • Fri and Sat are the local weekend, so arrive early at the wadis.
